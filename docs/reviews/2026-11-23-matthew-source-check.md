## Limits

- The sources are old reference works (Thayer's, Strong's, Whiston's Josephus), the Mishnah in Sefaria's English, and Wikipedia. They show a claim is standard; they are not current scholarship.
- The reviewers' source quotations came through a page reader that returns short excerpts. The lead re-checked four of them (Josephus, Antiquities 17.8.1 and 18.1.4; Mishnah Gittin 9:10; the hosted Didache 7:1) and read every proposed edit and outline note. The other quotations were not re-read.
- Each reviewer was one pass over seven chapters with a stated bar ("a careful editor would ask: says who?"). A claim below that bar may remain.
- The 82 claims marked `text` rest on the hosted World English Bible or the site's tagged Greek. Where the hosted English follows a different Greek reading from the tagged edition (3:11, 6:1, 11:19, 24:36), the guide now says so.
- Readings marked as readings were not adjudicated.
- The drafter of these guides is not recorded. Commit 52b2d572 of 2026-08-04, which rewrote all 28, names no model.
