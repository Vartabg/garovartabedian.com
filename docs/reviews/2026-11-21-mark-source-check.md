# Mark chapter guides: source check, 2026-11-21

**Status: applied on 2026-11-22.** Garo ruled to apply all three groups: the
30 corrections, the 18 softer wordings and the 30 context-line edits. The
tables below keep the wording as it stood at the baseline beside what
replaced it. Mark is still not bound to a rubric.

It follows the grounding review of the same day
(`2026-11-21-mark-grounding-review.md`), which left Mark's language and
background claims unverified.

## What was checked

- The 16 Mark chapter guides as they stand at `master` `f60a4990`.
- Every place the two readers flagged in the grounding review (163 places
  outside the context lines), plus every sentence in the guides that makes a
  claim about Greek, Hebrew, Aramaic or Latin (92 sentences), plus the 32
  audience and date lines.
- The lead grouped these into the claims listed below. A claim can cover
  more than one flagged place.

## How

- **Hosted text.** Word claims were run against the tagged Greek of Mark
  (`public/scriptures/original/MRK`), the tagged Hebrew, and the hosted
  English. Cross-references were read in the hosted World English Bible.
- **Sources.** For history, law, geography and word meanings, a helper
  fetched the passage and quoted it: Josephus (Whiston's translation), the
  Mishnah and Talmud (Sefaria), Thayer's and Strong's lexicons, Smith's,
  Easton's and the International Standard Bible Encyclopedia, Eusebius,
  Irenaeus, and Wikipedia where nothing older would load. Each helper was
  told to quote only what the page showed and to write "not found"
  otherwise.
- **Lead.** Claude Fable 5.2 chose the claims, set the verdicts, and re-read
  the passages behind the proposed corrections: Josephus, *Antiquities*
  17.10.10, 15.7.10 and 12.5.4; *War* 4.3.9, 4.5.2 and 4.8.3; Mishnah Yoma
  8:6, Shabbat 7:2 and Shekalim 6:5; the Thayer's entries for *metanoeo*,
  *rabbi*, *korban*, *hypokrites* and *tekton*; and the pages on Codex
  Bobiensis and Gehenna.
- **Trial run.** The proposed wording was applied to a scratch copy of the
  16 guides and the guide checks were run under the guide-v3 limits. No
  chapter gained a failure.

## Context lines (30 edits, one rule)

The rule: say what the Mark briefing says, no more firmly.

- **Date, all 16 chapters.** "Somewhere in the AD 50s–70s" becomes "Most
  scholars say the mid-AD 60s to shortly after 70; earlier dates are
  defended". Anything after that phrase in chapters 11, 13 and 16 stays.
- **Audience, 14 chapters.** Lines that open "Greek-speaking readers
  outside Judea" gain the briefing's "Likely". Lines that say "Non-Jewish
  readers" or "mostly not Jewish" take the briefing's wording instead. The
  remark in chapter 5 that Mark's readers "already keep" pigs is dropped.
  Chapters 13 and 15 word their line differently and are left alone.

## Limits of this check

- Several sources are old reference works (Thayer's 1889, Smith's 1863,
  Easton's 1897, the 1915 encyclopedia) or Wikipedia. They show that a
  claim is standard, or where it comes from. They are not current
  scholarship.
- The classical Greek lexicon, Pliny, Cicero and Plutarch would not load,
  so the claims that leaned on them are marked partly or open.
- Josephus was read in Whiston's chapter numbering; the modern section
  numbers were not checked.
- Four journal articles are known here only from abstracts and summaries
  (Hart 1952, Barr 1988, McIver 1994, and the work on Mark 4:12).
- The lead re-read the passages behind the corrections. The helpers'
  quotes for claims marked "source" were not all re-read.
- "Reading" rows were not adjudicated. They are judgments for a reviewer.
- This file does not show that the guides are free of other slips.
