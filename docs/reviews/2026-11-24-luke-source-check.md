## Limits

- The sources are old reference works (Thayer's, Strong's, Whiston's Josephus), the Mishnah in Sefaria's English, and Wikipedia. They show a claim is standard; they are not current scholarship.
- The reviewers' source quotations came through a page reader that returns short excerpts, and for some long pages a summary. The lead read every proposed edit and outline note but did not re-read the quotations.
- Where a guide sentence names an ancient writer the reviewers did not read directly (for example Ovid, Suetonius or Augustine through a reference page), the `basis` says where the statement was read.
- The Meta reader's subscription quota ran out partway through this book. A third outside model, `beam-free` through opencode, read the chapters it missed, after the main review; its findings that no claim already covered were checked in a late ledger.
- Each reviewer was one pass with a stated bar ("a careful editor would ask: says who?"). A claim below that bar may remain.
- Readings marked as readings were not adjudicated.
- The drafter of these guides is not recorded; the commit that rewrote them names no model.
- One guard line in chapter 22, on not disparaging the Jewish Passover, was kept by Garo's ruling; the proposed trim is listed as proposed and not applied.
