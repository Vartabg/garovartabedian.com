A translation switch exposed a data-integrity bug in my offline reader.

On garovartabedian.com, switching from the World English Bible to the King James Version could update the selected edition before the replacement text finished loading. During that gap, saving a chapter could pair the previous wording with the new translation's label.

The fix:
• Clear stale text as soon as the translation changes.
• Keep saving unavailable until the selected text is ready.
• Ignore responses from superseded requests.
• Show an explicit error when loading fails.

The regression tests cover delayed requests, failed requests, shared links, and reopening the saved chapter offline. They check the actual wording and edition together.

This is the kind of troubleshooting I enjoy: reproduce the failure, understand the state transition, repair it, and verify what the person using the product actually receives.
