# Independent plan review

Baseline f9d4181670f7b2991181cf28ccce7b57b404c63a. Fresh-context reviewer Dewey reviewed the original plan (SHA-256 b5e5e561a3ee47cbb2c0fe2a17504b25a032a0a572374ceddcf8096d8b2c9eed) and the source files in its reading list. The reviewer found five material gaps: no section-level denominator; omitted legacy and excerpt sources; hash integrity conflated with transcription and speaker accuracy; tests could pass with omitted comparisons; and item versus shared-catalog failure behavior was unclear.

All five findings were accepted. The revised plan adds a section-by-section checked-in ledger and independent source review; expands the reading list; checks named editions, scanned pages, speakers and editorial marks independently of hashes; requires independently reviewed expected comparisons and negative tests; and defines item and shared-catalog failures separately. The same reviewer completed a targeted re-review of revised plan SHA-256 377efe74c98614b84ce6b1184502c5f52ede5fac226c9b1eacfc7fb7c6df1edb and found no residual design blockers. The reviewer did not claim that the inventory or implementation had already been completed.

The independent hosted-text reviewer checked 48 new or expanded Lightfoot selections against the supplied CCEL transcription, then compared them with the 1891 printed edition. A separate targeted design review rejected rewriting the full library without accounting for semantic search, source identity, dependent hashes, and recovery from partial import. The revised design instead uses bounded, explicitly labeled corrected Parables excerpts and preserves the unchanged Early library and indexes. The reviewer accepted that direction with requirements for separately labeled original-reader and scan links, independent publication status, removal of unused traced files, and staged and failure checks; those requirements are part of the implementation. The original printings were not collated against the 1956 reprint, and the point where each difference entered the locally acquired CCEL-based transcription is unknown.

An implementation re-review found that duplicate catalog records could advertise a broken original-reader link; availability now requires one catalog record by ID and published status, with a negative test. The re-review also observed that deferring an entire edition makes its ordinary hosted selections unavailable and prevents the existing staged gate from approving the release. That gate intentionally requires all ordinary selections to load; this change only makes corrected excerpts independently readable and does not weaken release validation for an edition-wide withdrawal.
