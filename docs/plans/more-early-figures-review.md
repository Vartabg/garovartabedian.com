## Source collation

The selected Lightfoot library blocks (Barnabas 11 and 16; 1 Clement 25–26 and 48; Diognetus 7 and 12) were compared as normalized text with the CCEL Lightfoot XML at https://ccel.org/ccel/l/lightfoot/fathers.xml, with notes excluded: every selected block matched that witness. The Didache 16 legacy chapter was compared with the 1886 Riddle chapter at https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Ante-Nicene_Fathers/Volume_VII/The_Teaching_of_the_Twelve_Apostles/The_Teaching_of_the_Twelve_Apostles/Chapter_XVI. The Barnabas chapter was checked against the ANF translation at https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0124.htm, noting its modernized pronouns. The three James passages were checked against the PDF scan pages specified in the plan; token alignment was also checked against OCR of the printed scan. The comparison revealed a missing editorial <garlands> in the online hymn transcription, which was restored from the print edition. The Paris manuscript and U omission notes were checked on pp. 428–429. These checks establish the selected transcription and witness, not the historical truth of the writings' claims.
