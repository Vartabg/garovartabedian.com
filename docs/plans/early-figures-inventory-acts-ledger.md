# Acts of Thomas: section-by-section coverage ledger

Review date: 2026-11-01. Scope: Acts only. Repository: `repository checkout`.

## Method and status

Read James's complete Acts of Thomas, §§1–170, in consecutive, bounded PDF text-extraction chunks, with no output truncation: [M. R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (1924), pp. 364–438](https://www.biblicalstudies.org.uk/pdf/e-books/james_m-r/apocryphal-new-testament_james.pdf). Consulted the [Early Christian Writings transcription](https://www.earlychristianwritings.com/text/actsthomas.html) for accessible section citations and difficult passages. The scan is the controlling source where the web transcription drops editorial supplements or variant notes. Read the complete text, rather than only the titles, of all 39 current Acts excerpt files in `content/jesus/early-excerpts/` referenced by `public/jesus/early-figures.json`, and compared their actual starts, ends and internal content with the source. Inventory snapshot SHA-256: `c531667e18c136b47c4913ac866c2c66c2cbb9cda7a386679d47ae631f9366d3`.

The inventory changed during the review. In this snapshot the former §4-only wedding excerpt now covers §§4–5, and §§27, 39, 46–49, 50, 76, 91–92, 127, 133 and 158 have entries. Earlier claims that §§5, 39, 46–49, 91–92, or 133 were absent no longer describe this snapshot. The source and repository were read-only; this handoff file is the sole requested write.

**Key:** Selected = the cited excerpt actually includes the stated source text. Partial = part of the section is selected or a closely related figure is selected elsewhere. Candidate = a possible addition or explicit connection, not a claim it must be added. No candidate = no further developed figure/comparison recommended from that section, even if the section contains incidental metaphor, a miracle, or ordinary Gospel-sounding wording. Speaker is given where relevant; references to Jesus are to Jesus as portrayed within Acts. All Gospel comparisons are thematic or verbal, not claims of literary dependence.

## Handoff priorities and limits

1. **Most promising newly unselected material:** §15 groom's prayer; §34–35 youth's lost/found and darkness/light testimony; §60–61 Thomas's abandonment and true-fruit prayer; §87–88 desert/temple and indwelling exchange; §121 oil invocation; and §165 numerical interpretation. §29's creditor exchange is compact and its referent is unclear; present it as a bounded, tentative candidate. §136 is largely overlapping, and §144 / §167 are chiefly boundary and witness questions.
2. The 39-entry inventory is a snapshot, not an assertion about a deployed site or subsequent edits. Some excerpts overlap, notably §48. “Selected” means the relevant passage is physically in the current excerpt, not that every image has its own title, link, or interpretation.
3. James's edition is a historical translation with editorial supplements and Greek/Syriac witness choices, not a critical collation. He notes major textual differences at §§83–85, 132, 144–148 and 167; the hymn survives in limited witnesses and James's supplements and variant notes matter. PDF extraction contains OCR errors, so names and ambiguous words were checked against the scan's context and the web transcription where useful; the latter itself omits some bracketed words. No Greek or Syriac manuscript was independently collated.
4. Passage-by-passage review and the explicitly stated selection threshold do not establish exhaustive certainty. Gospel connections describe similarities and differences, not proven dependence. Some content conveys ancient ascetic claims and depictions of disability or gender; selecting it should retain speaker and context rather than endorse it.
