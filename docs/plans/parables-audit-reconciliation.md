## Gospel explanations already present

The sower explanations (Matthew 13:18–23, Mark 4:13–20, Luke 8:11–15), weeds explanation (Matthew 13:36–43), net explanation (Matthew 13:49–50) and defilement explanations (Matthew 15:15–20, Mark 7:17–23) were already present in account explanation fields before this reconciliation; the earlier audit incorrectly called them missing. John 7:39 is displayed as the Gospel narrator’s comment, not attributed to Jesus.
