# Five early writings in the Parables room

## Goal and scope
Add the passages identified in the preceding research from the Epistula Apostolorum, 2 Clement, Gospel of Philip, Gospel of Truth, and Shepherd of Hermas. They are historical writings outside the canon, not Scripture. Show the full selected passages in the room, link their real reader anchors, and identify the speakers or narrators accurately. Do not claim a passage is an independent quotation of Jesus or a direct parallel when the text gives only an interpretation or a shared image.

- Epistula Apostolorum chapter 43 (James 1924 existing `/early/epistula-apostolorum` reader): the risen Jesus and disciples discuss the ten virgins; place beside Matthew 25:1–13 and note differences. Retain narrator/disciples as well as Jesus. Read the existing legacy primary JSON, not the library's commentary-only record.
- 2 Clement 5:2–4 (Lightfoot): lambs among wolves and the dialogue between Peter and Jesus; add Matthew 10:16 and Luke 10:3 as biblical figures and show 2 Clement alongside them, with explicit note about the added dialogue.
- Gospel of Philip, Becoming Christians paragraphs 5, 11 and 12 (Mattison): pearl (author's image), dyer's work (Lord narrated/speaking), and the blind and sighted in darkness (Savior's dialogue). Speaker labels must distinguish the author from characters in the passages.
- Gospel of Truth: sheep paragraphs 1–2 (author's interpretation, compare with existing lost sheep, including Thomas); jars paragraphs 1–2; nightmares paragraph(s) in their complete section. Attribute to the author, not to a quotation of Jesus.
- Shepherd of Hermas: elm and vine (Another Parable paragraphs 1–10), vineyard and servant (same section paragraphs 27–37), and the tower (Vision 3 paragraphs 13–21), described as Hermas's vision with the woman/Church and the Shepherd, not Jesus speaking. Supply an exact passage link, note selected ranges when the larger explanations continue elsewhere.
